Learning Tips
01

Build small projects daily. Even 20 minutes of hands-on work beats hours of passive reading. Small wins compound fast.

02

Embrace error messages. Debugging is how you actually learn. Read the error carefully — it's telling you exactly what to fix.

03

Learn by doing, not just watching. Copy the example, break it, then fix it. That process locks in the knowledge.

04

Teach what you learn. Explain a concept to a friend (or just out loud to yourself). If you can't explain it simply, study it more.

05

Stay consistent, not intense. 30 minutes every day is far more powerful than a 6-hour marathon on the weekend.
